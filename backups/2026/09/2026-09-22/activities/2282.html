第一階段公費疫苗施打對象

  • 醫事及衛生防疫相關人員 
  • 65歲以上者(民國50年12月31日前生)
  • 55歲以上原住民
  • 安養、長期照顧（服務）等機構之受照顧者及其所屬工作人員
  • 滿6個月以上至國小入學前幼兒(攜帶兒童健康手冊)
  • 孕婦(攜帶婦幼健康手冊，需影印封面)
  • 具有潛在疾病者，包括19-64歲高風險慢性病人(具糖尿病、慢性肝病(含肝硬化)、心血管疾病、慢性肺病、腎臟疾病及免疫低下(HIV感染者)等疾病(請提供就醫紀錄影本或領藥影本)
  • BMI≧30者。
  • 罕見疾病患者及重大傷病患者(健保快易通 APP 記錄或領有重大傷病證明紙卡者)
  • 6個月內嬰兒之雙親或實際扶養者(攜帶嬰兒出生證明或戶口名簿、照顧者之身分證及簽立簽結書，需影印)
  • 幼兒園托育人員、托育機構專業人員及居家托育人員（保母）(需開立在職證明)
  •  國小、國中、高中、高職、五專一至三年級學生(攜帶補接種通知單及健保卡)
  •  禽畜相關及動物防疫相關人員

Phase 1 Vaccination Recipients

  • Medical, health, and epidemic prevention personnel
  • Individuals aged 65 and above (born on or before December 31, 1961)
  • Indigenous people aged 55 and above
  • Care recipients and staff of nursing homes and long-term care (service) facilities
  • Children from 6 months old to pre-elementary school age (must bring the Child Health Manual)
  • Pregnant women (must bring the Maternal and Child Health Booklet; a photocopy of the cover is required)
  • Individuals with underlying medical conditions, including high-risk chronic patients aged 19-64 with diseases such as diabetes, chronic liver disease (including cirrhosis), cardiovascular disease, chronic lung disease, kidney disease, and immunocompromised conditions (e.g., HIV patients) (please provide a photocopy of medical records or prescription receipts)
  • Individuals with a BMI ≥ 30
  • Patients with rare diseases and catastrophic illnesses (must have records in the NHI Express APP or hold a physical catastrophic illness certificate card)
  • Parents or actual guardians of infants under 6 months old (must bring the infant's birth certificate; a photocopy is required)
  • Preschool childcare personnel, childcare facility professionals, and in-home childcare providers/nannies (proof of employment is required)
  • Elementary, junior high, senior high, and vocational high school students, as well as students in grades 1 to 3 of five-year junior colleges (must bring the make-up vaccination notice and NHI card)
  • Poultry/livestock-related workers and animal epidemic prevention personnel

注意事項:

1.公費疫苗施打品牌依據桃園市衛生局當日供貨為準。

2.公費流感疫苗施打對象當日，可以同時接受新冠疫苗注射。

3.境外人士要有居留證及健保卡，並符合公費資格，才可以施打公費疫苗。

4.注射新冠疫苗不用攜帶小黃卡。

Important Notes:

  1. The brand of the government-funded vaccine will depend on the supply provided by the Taoyuan City Health Bureau on the day.
  2. Eligible recipients of the government-funded flu vaccine can also receive the COVID-19 vaccine on the same day.
  3. Foreign nationals must have a residence permit and National Health Insurance card, and meet the criteria for government-funded vaccines in order to receive the free vaccine.
  4. The COVID-19 vaccine can be administered without the need to bring the yellow vaccination card.

主要成分：含有 SARS-CoV-2 變異株棘蛋白之單價 mRNA 疫苗。

接種對象：

  • 65歲以上民眾
  • 55歲以上原住民(出示戶籍名簿或戶口謄本等可證明原住民族身分之文件)
  • 安養、長期照顧(服務)等機構之受照顧者及其所屬工作人員
  • 孕婦：已領取國民健康署「孕婦健康手冊」之懷孕婦女 ，若尚未發給孕婦健康手冊 ，則可檢附診斷證明書
  • 滿 6 個月以上高風險對象，括含符合公費流感疫苗高風險對象條件(高風險慢性病人、BMI≧30者、罕見病病患者及重大傷病患者) ，以及結核病、失能(注意力不足及過動症、腦性麻痺、先天性缺陷、發展或學習障礙、脊髓損傷) 、精神病病(情緒障礙、思覺失調症)、失智症患
  • 醫事及衛生防疫相關人員
  • 幼兒園托育人員 托育機構專業人員及居家托育人員(保母)(開立在職證明)
  • 6 個月內嬰兒之雙親或實際扶養者：攜帶嬰兒出生證明或戶口名簿、照顧者之身分證及簽立簽結書，需影印
  • 從未接種 COVID-19 疫苗之滿 6 個月至未滿 6 歲幼兒

 

Eligible Groups for Vaccination

  • Individuals aged 65 and above

  • Indigenous people aged 55 and above (must present a household certificate or household registration transcript as proof of indigenous status)

  • Care recipients and staff of nursing homes and long-term care (service) facilities

  • Pregnant women: Pregnant women who have received the "Maternal and Child Health Booklet" from the Health Promotion Administration; if the booklet has not yet been issued, a medical diagnostic certificate can be provided

  • High-risk individuals aged 6 months and above, including those who meet the high-risk criteria for the publicly funded influenza vaccine (high-risk chronic patients, individuals with a BMI ≥ 30, patients with rare diseases, and patients with catastrophic illnesses), as well as individuals with tuberculosis, disabilities (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD), cerebral palsy, congenital defects, developmental or learning disabilities, spinal cord injuries), psychiatric disorders (mood disorders, schizophrenia), and dementia patients

  • Medical, health, and epidemic prevention personnel

  • Preschool childcare personnel, childcare facility professionals, and in-home childcare providers/nannies (proof of employment is required)

  • Parents or actual guardians of infants under 6 months old: Must hold the infant's birth certificate or a household registration certificate showing the completed newborn registration

  • Children aged 6 months to under 6 years old who have never received a COVID-19 vaccine
