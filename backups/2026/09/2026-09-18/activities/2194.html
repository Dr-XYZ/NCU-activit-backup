2026國立中央大學秋季校園徵才暨國際生就業媒合會即將登場，現場集結 7 間企業、 1 個校內單位與履歷健檢與職涯諮詢服務展位，提供企業職缺資訊及面對面交流機會，歡迎同學把握機會，探索更多職涯可能！✨

研發處產學營運中心 NCU - Center for Academia and Industry Collaboration
履歷健檢與職涯諮詢服務 Resume Check-up and Career Consultation


🗓️活動時間：2026/9/18（五）10:00-15:00
📍活動地點：教學研究綜合大樓1樓活動空間
📑活動形式：

  1. 本活動採自由參加，歡迎事先報名，亦可於活動當天自由參與；本校學生當天入場請攜帶學生證並刷卡入場。
  2. 現場設有企業招募及人才媒合攤位，歡迎同學與企業代表直接交流，了解職缺資訊及招募需求。
  3. 現場另設置「履歷健檢服務」，學生可事先預約或於活動當日於服務臺報名參與。事先預約者享有優先服務，現場報名者則依候位順序安排，歡迎同學把握機會，提前為求職履歷做好準備！
  4. 請同學至 Google 雲端硬碟瀏覽企業職缺資訊，相關職缺將陸續更新，歡迎同學持續關注！https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1txDkX1xyicsiB1IXqUoc7ygJ5Bssjbpe?usp=drive_link
  5. 部分企業提供制式履歷供同學投遞，請同學務必留意各企業的履歷格式及相關要求，並於投遞前再次確認資料是否完整、格式是否正確。

📌活動時數：
完成簽到並填寫活動回饋表者，可獲得 1 點職涯活動助學金點數（適用「安心就學計畫」）。符合安心就學計畫資格的同學，請於當天活動結束後至服務臺領取。


💡小提醒：記得準備好履歷，9月18日一起來與企業面對面交流，了解更多職缺資訊，探索屬於你的職涯機會吧！祝大家求職順利！✨


📢 【9/18】Looking for a job, interested in company openings, or exploring your future career path? This is your chance!

The 2026 NCU Autumn Campus Talent Recruitment & International Talent Maching Event is coming soon! This year’s event brings together 7 companies, 1 campus-unit, as well as Resume Check-up and Career Consultation services. Students will have the opportunity to learn about job openings and communicate directly with company representatives. Don’t miss the chance to explore more career possibilities! ✨


🏢 Participating Booths:
Industrial Technology Research Institute
WPORT
Hotfire Digital Information Co., Ltd.
FuSheng Precision
Wiwynn Corporation
United Microelectronics Corporation
Lion Group
Steering Committee for the Test Of Proficiency – Huayu
NCU - Center for Academia and Industry Collaboration
Resume Check-up and Career Consultation


🗓️ Date & Time: September 18, 2026 (Fri.), 10:00–15:00
📍 Venue: 1F Event Space, Teaching & Research Building
📑 Event Format:

  1. You are welcome to pre-register, or simply join us on the day of the event. NCU students are welcome to join freely. Please bring your student ID card and swipe it upon entry.
  2. Company recruitment and talent-matching booths will be available on site. Students are encouraged to speak directly with company representatives to learn more about job openings and recruitment requirements.
  3. A Resume Check-up and Career Consultation service will also be available. Students may make an appointment in advance or register at the service desk on the event day. Students with advance reservations will receive priority service, while walk-in participants will be served according to the waiting order. Take advantage of this opportunity to improve your resume and prepare for your job search!
  4. Please visit the Google Drive folder below to view job openings and recruitment information from participating companies. Information will be updated continuously, so please check back regularly:
    https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1txDkX1xyicsiB1IXqUoc7ygJ5Bssjbpe?usp=drive_link
  5. Some companies require applicants to use their designated resume templates. Please carefully review each company’s resume format and application requirements, and make sure all information is complete and correctly formatted before submitting your resume.

📌 Mandarin Course Reward Card:

Attend and complete the feedback form to receive one stamp on your Mandarin Course Reward Card.

💡 Reminder: Don’t forget to prepare your resume! Join us on September 18 to meet company representatives face-to-face, learn more about job opportunities, and explore your future career possibilities. Good luck with your job search! ✨
