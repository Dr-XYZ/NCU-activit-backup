學生的注意力往往在課程進行到一半時開始下降，而常見的解釋不外乎教材太難或課程太長。本場次主張，授課的規劃方式與內容同樣重要，而在 EMI 課堂中，跟上進度本身就相當吃力，此時敘事結構就能發揮作用，它讓學生知道這堂課要走向何處，以及答案為何重要。我們將探討科學教學如何運用敘事，又不至於流於說故事。

 

Student attention tends to drop partway through a lecture, and the usual explanations are that the material is too difficult or the lecture is too long. This session makes the case that the shape of the delivery matters just as much as the content. In an EMI classroom, where following along is already immensely challenging, narrative structure does real work: it tells students where a session is going and why the answer matters. We'll look at how science teaching can use narrative without turning into storytelling.

在 EMI 課堂中，有些學生很少開口，而一般習慣將此視為學生本身的問題。本場次要問的是：這是否是該檢視的地方？我們將從教師與學生的第一手經驗出發，檢視三種再平常不過的授課慣例：向全班提問、等待時間過短，以及在同儕面前說英語所帶來的暴露感。無論站在講台前的是誰，以上三者狀況都同樣地製造沉默。慣例是可以改變的，我們也將探討可以用什麼來取代它們。

 

Some students in EMI classes rarely speak, and it's common to treat that as a student problem. This session asks whether that's the right place to look. Drawing on firsthand accounts from professors and students, we'll examine three ordinary lecture conventions: whole-class questions, short wait times, and the exposure of speaking English in front of peers. Each produces silence fairly reliably, whoever is at the front of the room. Conventions can be changed, and we'll look at what to replace them with.
